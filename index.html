Maximum privacy

Manual JSON · on your device

Your data lives only on your phone or PC. Nothing is sent to the cloud.

  • Nothing uploaded, ever, in this mode
  • Works fully offline
  • Back up and move devices with a JSON file you export yourself
  • Logging out clears the device, so download a backup first
Every device, always current

Cloud sync · encrypted

Your data syncs to your own account, so your phone, tablet and PC always agree.

  • Encrypted in transit with HTTPS/TLS
  • Records locked to your account at the database level
  • Changes saved straight away, other devices catch up within about a minute
  • Switch to device-only whenever you like

See how your data travels

How the cloud keeps you safe

When you choose cloud sync, these safeguards protect your data and your sign-in.

Locks that live on your device

Even if someone picks up your phone or sits at your PC, your numbers stay hidden.

App Lock

Face ID, fingerprint or passkey on phones, and a PC Lock passkey on desktop. Your PC passkey is kept only as a salted hash, never in plain text.

Privacy Screen

The moment you click away, switch apps, minimise or open recent apps, a solid cover hides every amount. It lifts when you come back, or asks you to unlock after a delay you choose.

Extra PIN for sensitive controls

Admin controls sit behind a separate PIN, stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash. Five wrong tries lock it for 30 seconds.
